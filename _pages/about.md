Hi! I'm Yeeseok. I'm a master's student at The University of Tokyo.
I currently work on **computational social choice**, under the supervision of <a href='https://sites.google.com/site/eigarashayumi/home'>Prof. Ayumi Igarashi</a>. I am also a part-time researcher in the <a href='https://aip.riken.jp/labs/math_intel/computational_social_choice/'>Computational Social Choice Team</a> at <a href='https://aip.riken.jp/'>RIKEN AIP</a>.

My interest lies in committee elections, fair division, and other fields in the intersection of economics and computation. I am also interested in the broad field of combinatorial optimization.

I’m always happy to hear from people interested in collaboration, so please feel free to reach out by email!
